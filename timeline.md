## Dependencies and fallback
Data availability determines the final crop and market selection. If a preferred dataset is incomplete or inconsistent, narrow the geography/time range or select another crop rather than forcing an unreliable merge. Forecasting should be omitted or clearly marked as not feasible if the series is too short or irregular.
